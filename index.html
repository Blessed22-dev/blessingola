You wrote a beautiful story. Now it needs pictures that sell it.

Children choose books with their eyes first, and parents buy with theirs. These are the problems I hear most from authors before we start.

  • The character looks different on every page. Kids notice. I build a character sheet first, so your hero stays the same from cover to last spread.
  • The art looks generic or AI-made. Every illustration is drawn for your story, with expressions and details your young readers will spot again and again.
  • The cover doesn't stand out on Amazon. Your cover is designed to read clearly at thumbnail size, where most buyers first see it.
  • KDP keeps rejecting your files. Bleed, trim size, margins and spine width are set up correctly from the very first page.

Questions

Children's book illustration FAQ

How much does it cost to illustrate a children's book?

It depends on how many pages you have, how detailed each scene is and whether you need a cover and formatting too. Send your manuscript and page count and you'll get an exact quote, usually within 24 hours.

How long does children's book illustration take?

Most picture books take a few weeks, depending on page count and how quickly sketches are approved. You'll get a clear timeline before any work starts.

Will my character look the same on every page?

Yes. I start with a character design sheet showing your hero from different angles and with different expressions, and use it for every page.

Can you design the cover and format the book for Amazon KDP too?

Yes. Cover design and KDP formatting are offered as add-ons, so your illustrations, cover and interior all match and are ready to upload.

What trim sizes do you work with?

Any size KDP and IngramSpark support, including popular picture book sizes like 8.5 × 8.5 in and 8.5 × 11 in. Not sure which to pick? I'll recommend one for your story.

Do I own the rights to the illustrations?

Yes. After final payment, you can publish, print and sell your book with the illustrations.

Can I see sketches before the final illustrations?

Always. You approve rough sketches for every page before I move to final colour.

How do we work together if I live in another country?

Everything happens online. We talk on WhatsApp or email, and you can also hire me through Upwork if you prefer to pay on a platform.
